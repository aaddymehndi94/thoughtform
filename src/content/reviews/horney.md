# Karen Horney: editorial direction

Twenty cards, four connected five-card threads. The newcomer moves from the idealized image to its demands, then to defensive relationships and the possibility of development. Each card has one distinction, an original everyday example, a specific limit on interpretation, and a question. The sequences are introductions to a historical psychoanalytic framework, not diagnostic tools.

For each lesson I considered three approaches: a purely typographic contrast, a symbolic diagram, and a changing concrete situation. I chose the latter when it could retain the actual task/person while exposing an invisible demand. For feasibility, conflicting demands, and retrospective judgment, a diagram makes the particular relation clearer than another person-at-a-desk scene. Rejected decorative arrows and identical actor layouts with substituted labels.

## Teaching points and visual mechanisms

- Idealized self: a private portrait makes ordinary fatigue unacceptable. An unanswered message stays visible beside the portrait. Stills compare available energy and allow a helper’s own need.
- Actual/real: present conditions and living potential belong to one person. A small plant is compared with a framed finish; light becomes a condition. Stills show curiosity beside a draft, and tools beside practice.
- Glory: work becomes proof of identity. The stage rises while the score remains. Stills separate finishing from another proof and show concrete craft feedback.
- Pride: infallibility makes correction threatening. A badge grows into a brittle shield. Stills compare evidence with an absolute claim and place suggestions on a common table.
- Claims: an unspoken bargain is experienced as binding. The other signature is visibly absent. Stills distinguish signed promises and fair payment from admiration.
- Shoulds: demands refuse to hear the actual person. Commands crowd a task. Stills explain task/time negotiation and finite available energy.
- Feasibility: a missing condition does not become a worker’s defect. Two work blocks cannot fit one hour. Stills separate scope/time/help and show understaffing.
- Crossfire: two total demands cannot share a finite evening. Time blocks overlap, then specific requests allow judgment. Stills show one clock and added support.
- Retrospective shoulds: later information must not be lent silently to the earlier decision. Facts move between two calendars. Stills distinguish concealed/ignored warnings and present repair.
- Ideals: a value allows work and learning; a dictate requires instant completion. A path becomes a wall and returns to steps. Stills retain repair and effort.
- Basic anxiety: safety-seeking has a relational origin. An unpredictable door changes the child’s position. Stills retain relationship and later support.
- Toward: compulsory helpfulness can remove the helper’s own place. Cups crowd a shared tray. Stills distinguish a freely offered favour and two preferences.
- Against: assuming competition prevents recognising collaboration. A work table becomes territory. Stills separate help/threat and combine contributions.
- Away: a useful boundary can cease to distinguish situations. The same door keeps out both noise and a friend’s gift. Stills show a wanted experience and boundary positions.
- Conflict: a dominant defence leaves competing aims attached. Three carriage handles remain through a reversal. Stills show submerged wishes and flexible responses.
- Self-hate: a missed task becomes a total verdict. One unticked box enlarges into a stamp; the task remains. Stills compare scales and return to a specific apology.
- Externalization: a private demand appears outside. A reflected sentence covers waiting people, then its source becomes visible. Stills separate kinds of evidence and keep real external pressure present.
- Alienation: expected preferences replace the person’s own response. A menu gains one modest personal item. Stills add interest and experiential discovery.
- Insight: naming a defence does not remove its function. A knot supports a shelter; alternative supports precede loosening. Stills retain experienced danger and examine recurrence.
- Growth: attention turns from proving a finished identity to living conditions. The imagined tree recedes; the actual plant changes modestly. Stills show a retained practice page and a shared world.
